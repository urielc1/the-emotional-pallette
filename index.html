One image.
More than one reading.

Color may catch your eye. Movement might hold it. Memory changes what happens next.

INTRODUCTION

Our group chose to explore the relationship between art and emotion because it reveals how humans process feelings, connect across perspectives, and experience meaning through creative expression. One interesting aspect of art is how a single piece can spark completely different emotional reactions depending on the viewr's background, mood, and upbringing.

Through this interactive website, we hope visitors will actively engage with different works and reflect on thier own emotional responses to see their interpretations compare to those of others. Ultimately, our goal is to show that art's emotional power cannot be fixed as it lives in the personal connection betwen us the viewer and the artpiece.

HOW TO USE THIS SITE

Look at each work before reading about it. Choose the feeling closest to your first response, then reveal a possible interpretation.

Your selections stay in this page. No account, storage, or diagnosis.
